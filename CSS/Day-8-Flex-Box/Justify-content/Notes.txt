Justify-Content:

Definition:Justify-content controls the alignment of flex items along the main axis.

Syntax:

.container{
    display:flex;
    justify-content:center;
}

Common values:

flex-start
center
flex-end
space-between
space-around
space-evenly

Important:

For flex-direction:row,the main axis is horizontal.

Therefore,Justify-content controls horizontal alignment.
